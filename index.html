Neurology Review & Differential Diagnosis Exclusions

Rigorous multidisciplinary evaluation excluding mimicking neurodegenerative syndromes and confounding spinal cord or canal pathologies.

1. Neurodegenerative Mimic Exclusions

Exclusion of primary neurodegenerative diseases causing gait and cognitive decline.

Alzheimer's Disease (AD) Early cortical amnesia, word-finding deficits, temporal lobe atrophy
Parkinson's Disease (PD) Resting 4-6Hz tremor, prominent cogwheel rigidity, marked asymmetry
Progressive Supranuclear Palsy (PSP) Vertical supranuclear gaze palsy, early backward falls, humming bird sign
Multiple System Atrophy (MSA) / CBD Severe dysautonomia, stridor, ataxia, corticobasal alien limb, apraxia
Dementia with Lewy Bodies (DLB) Visual hallucinations, REM sleep behavior disorder, fluctuating cognition
Vascular Dementia / Subcortical Binswanger Stepwise deterioration, focal neurologic signs, extensive lacunar infarcts

2. Spinal Cord & Canal Pathology Exclusions

Exclusion of spine disease mimicking or compounding gait impairment in the elderly.

Cervical Spondylotic Myelopathy (CSM) Hyperreflexia, positive Hoffman's/Babinski, spasticity, cord compression on MRI
Lumbar Spinal Canal Stenosis (LSS) Neurogenic claudication, pain relieved by forward flexion, sciatica
Peripheral Neuropathy / Proprioceptive Loss Glove/stocking sensory deficit, absent ankle jerks, impaired vibration sense

Pharmacotherapy & Non-Surgical CSF Modulation Rationale

Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibition: Acetazolamide (Diamox): Inhibits choroid plexus carbonic anhydrase, reducing CSF formation by up to 50%. Frequently employed as a temporary bridge in high-ICP LOVA decompensation or symptomatic morning headaches.
Metabolic Glymphatic Clearance: Metformin: Investigated for promoting astrocyte water balance, aquaporin-4 (AQP4) polarization, reducing periventricular neuroinflammation, and enhancing convective glymphatic waste clearance in hydrocephalus.
Diuretics & Symptom Control: Loop Diuretics & Beta-3 Agonists: Furosemide / Topiramate for intracranial pressure modulation; Mirabegron / Anticholinergics for urgency and urge incontinence symptoms in the Adams-Hakim triad.

Comprehensive Complication & Malfunction Taxonomy

Overdrainage Phenomena: Subdural hygroma, chronic subdural hematoma (CSDH), slit ventricle syndrome, low-pressure postural headache. Managed by raising differential or anti-gravity setting.
Hardware Malfunction Etiologies: Proximal ventricular catheter occlusion (choroid plexus ingrowth), valve mechanism clogging with high-protein debris, distal peritoneal catheter pseudocyst, catheter migration or fracture.
Infection & Extracranial: Staphylococcus epidermidis colonization, wound dehiscence over valve, abdominal visceral perforation, pleural effusion (in ventriculopleural shunts).
